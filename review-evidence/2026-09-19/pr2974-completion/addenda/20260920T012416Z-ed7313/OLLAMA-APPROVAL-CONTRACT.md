At published 3780, `scripts/ci/installed_native_ollama_probe.py` resolves a durable review and calls its subsequent `review_case` with `approval_reused=True`. The frozen fixture then expects allow and `approval_reuse_status=accepted`. Meanwhile, production `_command_reuse_is_payload_bound` in `hook_native_review_retry.py` intentionally admits only a small explicit set of payload-bound commands; Ollama is excluded. Script/interpreter/package invocations and unknown executables require stronger native source-bound identity. That restriction does not decide the command's semantic safety and cannot be relaxed solely to satisfy a stale fixture expectation.

The probe already reports `native_approval_consume_qualified=false` and `tool_invocation_mode=synthetic_policy_review_only`. Its password-bound mutation proofs authorize extension control changes; those are separate from a native one-use authorization to execute the reviewed command. Existing old 22-case Ollama report passes remain historical and do not establish modern native approved consumption or full contribution/update/rollback qualification.

The bounded local correction keeps the same strong-ID hook payload and current ACK, resolves the original approval through the genuine store path, and proves that the retry retains a matching native review receipt and a distinct durable pending approval without accepted compatibility reuse. It must retain all current phase/floor checks, contribution/package/runtime identities, generation advancement, password-bound control mutations, stale-revision rejection and the unchanged 400 ms readiness limit. It records a changed expected route with its own source and installed evidence; it does not retroactively relabel 3780's failure.

Any future claim that an Ollama command can proceed after native approval needs the actual executable/source-bound issue, revalidation and authenticated consume path, with replay, expiry, generation, stricter-policy and ambiguous-completion controls. Neither this fixture correction nor a successful extension-control mutation supplies that proof. RSP-080 and RSP-118 statuses and complete original objects remain unchanged.
